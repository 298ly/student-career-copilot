# Interview research and provenance

## Query design

Combine company alias, exact role, role family, internship or campus-hire status, year, interview round, and key JD skill. Search both exact-role reports and role-common practice material.

## Source roles

- Company and JD pages establish current role requirements.
- Nowcoder and similar interview reports provide noisy recollections of actual processes and questions.
- LeetCode provides structured practice; it does not prove a company asked a problem.
- Publicly indexed Xiaohongshu results may provide timely anecdotes; retain links and treat unsupported claims cautiously.
- The student's raw interview record is most personal but may still be incomplete.

## Required metadata

Record source ID, URL, title, platform, author if public and relevant, publication date, access date, claimed company, claimed role, question paraphrase, source type, and confidence.

## Confidence guidance

- High: student's contemporaneous record, official assessment, or multiple independent recent same-role reports.
- Medium: one detailed attributable report or repeated role-common pattern.
- Low: undated, promotional, second-hand, or weakly matched material.

## Copyright and access

Store links and brief paraphrases. Do not mirror full posts, paid lessons, or commercial question banks. Do not circumvent access controls or automate prohibited scraping. Let the user manually provide content that is inaccessible, while warning them not to share confidential interview material.
